Pinecone is a free Android reader for books, PDFs, comics and audiobooks, with Material 3 design.

Read EPUB, PDF and CBZ files, image publications and audiobook packages. Organize your library with spaces, shelves and tags; save reading positions, bookmarks, notes and highlights where supported.

Customize typography, spacing, colors and reading controls. Listen to audiobooks with playback speed controls. Import StarDict dictionaries for offline lookup. Switch supported PDFs to an on-device reflowable reading view; scanned pages remain images and OCR is not supported.

Create manual or scheduled local backups and restore your books, dictionaries, settings and reading progress. English and Indonesian interfaces are available.

Requires Android 14 or newer. Pinecone is licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later.
