Your books, documents, comics, and audiobooks deserve a comfortable home. Pinecone brings them together in a customizable Android library, ready for reading and listening on your device.

Bring your collection together
Import EPUB books, PDFs, CBZ comics, images, and audiobooks. Browse your library by cover, search titles and authors, and filter by format, reading status, or shelf.

Organize your way
Arrange titles in shelves and separate spaces, edit book details and covers, and organize multiple titles at once. Keep new imports in an Unsorted inbox or archive titles for later.

Read comfortably
Adjust fonts, text size, spacing, colors, and brightness. Read PDFs in their original layout or switch to a reflowable view generated on your device. Scanned pages remain images; OCR is not supported.

Remember and discover
Keep your place with reading progress, bookmarks, notes, and highlights where supported. Search inside EPUB books and PDFs, or import a StarDict dictionary for offline word lookup.

Listen and keep your library safe
Play audiobook packages and supported audio files, navigate chapters, and adjust playback speed. Create manual or scheduled local backups and restore your library, dictionaries, settings, and reading progress.

Choose a light or dark theme and an English or Indonesian interface. Requires Android 14 or newer.
